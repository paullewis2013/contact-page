About

I'm Paul Lewis, a fullstack engineer and product designer based in New York. I build digital-twin simulation software for communications-based train control, used on rail projects around the world. My usual stack is TypeScript and React on the front end, with C#, .NET, Python and GraphQL behind it. Recently I moved a legacy desktop simulator to a web platform with a GraphQL API, and more than doubled a simulator's capacity through threading work. Outside work I run a homelab on Proxmox, Docker and Tailscale.
